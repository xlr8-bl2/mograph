# Venue music monitoring by CMOs in Europe, North America and Oceania

Scope note: about 20 tool calls. Several fetches failed (OneMusic, PRS help pages and MusicTech returned 403 or 429; some pages rendered empty), so coverage is uneven. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from prior knowledge, not from a source fetched in this session. The report writer should treat them as leads to check, not as facts.

## Q1. Which societies monitor venues, and how? (inventory table)

### Takeaway
Monitoring based on recognition technology is concentrated in **clubs and discotheques** and in **DJ-led festival stages**. It is done with a small random sample of fixed boxes (roughly 50 to 120 venues per country), run by specialist vendors: DJ Monitor, Yacast/SoundAware, BMAT, Pro Conecta and Audoo. Bars, shops, restaurants and gyms are mostly handled through proxies such as radio or chart data, or through background-music provider data. The one clear exception is **Audoo**, whose meters are aimed at "cafes, pubs, restaurants, retailers" (UK, Australia, NZ, Ireland, Germany). Audoo is also the main vendor whose device is generally described as ambient (microphone) capture, although I could not confirm the capture method from a fetched source.

### Inferences
- The dominant European model for clubs is the same everywhere: a fixed box in a statistically chosen sample (80–120 venues), recording continuously or at times the venue does not know, with fingerprinting done centrally. This is the closest precedent for SoundProof's "sample of bars" design.
- Vendors are moving to monitoring during all opening hours. GEMA's 2025 SoundAware contract is the explicit example.
- A recurring outcome across CMOs is poor matching of electronic, white-label and remix material, and repertoire missing from reference databases. This is why GEMA (2016) and SUISA added member soundfile uploads. In Cameroon, local makossa, bikutsi and afro repertoire will need reference ingestion for the same reason.

### Gaps
- Precise current box counts for SACEM and SUISA, and SACEM's current vendor.
- Distribution rules quantifying how much club money is distributed from monitoring versus by analogy, for most CMOs.

## Q2. DJ hardware and software integrations (KUVO, rekordbox, Serato): who uses them, and how well do they work?

### Takeaway
"KUVO powered by DJ Monitor" (AlphaTheta and DJ Monitor) combines Direct Metadata Capture from networked Pioneer CDJ-3000/2000 decks with MRT fingerprinting. APRA AMCOS was first, reportedly using KUVO data in distributions from about 2018–19. PRS/PPL and BUMA ran pilots. No independent accuracy study was found. No Serato integration with any CMO was found.

### Cited Findings
- KUVO combines MRT with Direct Metadata Capture, which reads track data from music played through CDJ-3000/2000 units — [AlphaTheta](https://alphatheta.com/en/information/djs-and-clubs-back-kuvo-powered-by-dj-monitor-for-fair-royalties/); [Promotion Centre interview](https://promotioncentre.co.uk/blog/discover-how-kuvo-powered-by-dj-monitor-is-creating-a-fairer-music-rights-ecosystem/)
- APRA AMCOS was the first PRO to sign with Pioneer DJ for KUVO reports and to use them in distributions. A KUVO–BMAT partnership in 2019 supported AFEM's "Get Played Get Paid" campaign — [Pioneer DJ 2019](https://pioneerdj.com/en-us/news/2019/kuvo-and-bmat-partnership)
- Pilot programmes ran with PRS for Music/PPL (UK) and BUMA (NL) — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/news/kuvo-powered-by-dj-monitors-pilot-programs-set-to-revolutionize-fair-music-royalties/); [Decoded Magazine](https://www.decodedmagazine.com/alphatheta-announces-significant-progress-with-its-kuvo-powered-by-dj-monitor-initiative/)
- The device installed in the venue captures an encrypted audio fingerprint and matches it against a track database — [ekko.nl KUVO info sheet](https://ekko.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Artist_DJ_Agent-KUVO-info-sheet.pdf)
- PRS says the devices are installed in the venue, not on DJ equipment, so they do not affect the performance — [PRS M magazine](https://www.prsformusic.com/m-magazine/features/music-recognition-technology-mrt-djs-from-decks-to-data)
- Venue licence fees are unaffected by deployment — [Promotion Centre](https://promotioncentre.co.uk/blog/discover-how-kuvo-powered-by-dj-monitor-is-creating-a-fairer-music-rights-ecosystem/)

### Inferences
- DJ metadata capture is irrelevant to Cameroon bars where music comes from phones, USB sticks, YouTube or CDs. It only helps where Pioneer CDJs are networked, which is rare outside high-end clubs.

### Gaps
- No published success metrics for KUVO, such as the share of sets captured or the accuracy of metadata versus fingerprints.
- rekordbox software logs and Serato: no CMO integration found in this session.

## Q3. Microphone and ambient recognition versus line-in, and trials and results

### Takeaway
No fetched source explicitly states the connection method for GEMA, SACEM, SGAE, SUISA, PRS or DJ Monitor boxes. The only explicit statement found was the GEMA search summary saying the boxes are installed at the mixing desks, which implies a line-in feed from the mixer. Audoo is the main vendor marketed for bars, pubs, shops and restaurants that do not have a controllable DJ mixer. It is generally understood to be a small device that plugs into a wall socket and listens through a microphone. This is **[UNVERIFIED]**: my fetches of MusicTech and Music Ally failed. Audoo says no audio is stored or sent, only fingerprints. Its published result covers live-music venues: 21% of 623 h of live audio matched known works.

### Cited Findings
- GEMA: about 120 monitoring boxes "installed directly at the mixing desks" (search summary of GEMA pages; the fetched GEMA page itself did not state the connection) — [GEMA](https://www.gema.de/en/musikurheber/monitoring)
- Audoo meters are installed in "cafes, pubs, restaurants, retailers and other businesses" for PRS/PPL. The first commercial rollout was with APRA (AU), followed by NZ, with partnerships claimed with IMRO and GEMA — [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2023/08/09/audoo-strikes-music-monitoring-deal-with-prs-for-music-and-ppl/); [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/the-uks-prs-for-music-and-ppl-team-up-with-music-startup-audoo-for-public-performance-royalty-allocation/); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/audoo-profile-ryan-edwards-interview/)
- Audoo says its device is GDPR-compliant and that no audio is ever stored or sent from it — [Headliner Hub](https://www.headlinerhub.com/music-royalty-black-hole-artists-payments-audoo-solution.html)
- MVT/Audoo: of 623 h of grassroots live audio, 134 h (21%) were identified as known works. The second study runs Oct–Dec 2026 in about 120 UK venues (one article says 12) — [DMN](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/06/09/mvt-audoo-partnership-prs-proxies/); [Pollstar](http://news.pollstar.com/2026/06/11/music-venue-trust-audoo-to-assess-performance-royalty-distribution-in-uk-grassroots-sector/); [Headliner Hub MVT](https://headlinerhub.com/music-venue-trust-and-audoo-launch-royalty-distribution-study-across-grassroots-venues.html)
- The "Recognise The Music" campaign calls for wider use of audio-ID in processing performance royalties — [CMU via Chris Cooke](https://mynameischriscooke.com/article/cmu-news-recognise-the-music-campaign-calls-for-wider-embracing-of-audio-id-for-processing-performance-royalties/)
- PRS/DJ Monitor club and festival identification rate: 80–95% — [PRS](https://www.prsformusic.com/m-magazine/features/music-recognition-technology-mrt-djs-from-decks-to-data)

### Inferences
- Why line-in dominates in clubs (inferred, not sourced): the mixer feed is a clean signal unaffected by crowd noise and room acoustics, there is an obvious install point in the DJ booth, and it captures only the programmed music. Clubs are loud, so a microphone would also pick up MC talk-overs and crowd noise.
- Why microphone or ambient capture is used for bars and shops (inferred): many venues have no mixer, using phones, Bluetooth or consumer speakers, and a plug-and-forget device needs no technician. That fits Cameroon's bar context. The cost is lower signal-to-noise, which modern fingerprinting partly tolerates. Privacy concerns are handled by fingerprinting on the device, as Audoo claims.
- The 21% figure is about **live** performance, which is mostly original or unregistered music. It is not a measure of recognition accuracy for recorded music, so it should not be quoted as an accuracy figure for SoundProof.

### Gaps
- No fetched source states whether DJ Monitor, SoundAware/SoundNodes, BMAT (SGAE) or Pro Conecta boxes use line-in or a microphone. Vendor spec sheets are needed.
- BMAT microphone devices, "Vericast Venue", Soundmouse "Venue" and smartphone-app trials were not found in this session.
- PPL use of background-music provider data (for example Soundtrack Your Brand or Mood Media playlist logs) was not found in this session. **[UNVERIFIED]**: PPL/PRS are believed to use background-music supplier reports as distribution data.
- SOCAN's acquisitions (Audiam, MediaNet) relate to digital and online licensing, not venue monitoring **[UNVERIFIED]**. No SOCAN venue MRT programme was found.
- Not researched: SIAE, SABAM, AKM, the Nordic societies (Teosto, KODA, STIM, TONO), ZAiKS, OSA, Artisjus and SPA. **[UNVERIFIED leads]**: STIM, TONO and KODA have reportedly used DJ Monitor or other club recognition, and Teosto has run club-reporting pilots. SIAE is believed to rely mostly on self-reported programme returns ("borderò") for dance venues.
