# CIMFEST and the CIMFEST Hackathon (as of 21 November 2026)

## What is CIMFEST exactly (full name, organizer, history, past editions)?

### Takeaway
CIMFEST is the **Cameroon International Music Festival**, an annual week-long music and entertainment festival held in **Buea (and Limbe), South West Region, Cameroon**. It has run since 2021, and a 48-hour hackathon focused on the music and creative industry is part of the festival. There is no other plausible "CIMFEST hackathon". Search results for "CIMFEST 2026" outside Cameroon only found unrelated "CIM" events (CIM CONNECT mining expo, CIMIx Vienna, CIMAR), and none of them run a CIMFEST hackathon.

### Cited Findings
- Full name: Cameroon International Music Festival (CIMFEST), a one-week free music and entertainment event in Buea, celebrating Cameroonian and African music — [Music In Africa](https://musicinafrica.net/node/340498)
- The official site (cimfest.com) is titled "CIMFEST 2026 - The vibe of the year is Active". It says the festival has run since 2021 and claims 130K+ cumulative attendees, 500+ vendors, 200M+ FCFA in direct sales, 480+ performances and **"four hackathon editions"** — [cimfest.com](https://cimfest.com/)
- The site does not name the organizing entity. It is "© Cliq Empire" and "designed and managed by" Iknite Studio, with design, creative and strategy partners Iknite Studio, Cliq Empire and IkniteOS. Tickets are sold via Cliqkets and Awards voting runs on cliqvotes.com — [cimfest.com](https://cimfest.com/)
- Leadership: **Prince-Michael Enobi** (also spelled "Prince Mykel-ENOBI"), Promoter and President of CIMFEST. He is quoted saying "Our goal is to promote Cameroonian music and culture" — [Cameroon Tribune, 2 Dec 2025](http://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/74231/fr.html/cameroon-international-music-festival-entertainment-blends-with-business-in); [Africa.com, 2024](https://africa.com/cameroon-international-music-festival-cimfest-returns-for-its-3rd-edition-on-november-9-2024/)
- Mission: promote peace, economic re-emergence and resilience in Cameroon, especially the conflict-affected North West and South West Regions — [Africa.com](https://africa.com/cameroon-international-music-festival-cimfest-returns-for-its-3rd-edition-on-november-9-2024/); [Standard Media (Kenya)](https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/africa/article/2001535069/melody-as-remedy-how-music-festival-is-easing-conflict-pains-in-cameroon)
- 2024 edition: 9–17 November 2024, called the "3rd edition" by Africa.com — [Africa.com](https://africa.com/cameroon-international-music-festival-cimfest-returns-for-its-3rd-edition-on-november-9-2024/). Cameroon Tribune called the 2024 edition the "fourth edition", opening 10 Nov 2024 at Molyko Omnisport Stadium and Buea Mountain Hotel — [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/67999/en.html/details_2). **The edition numbering conflicts between sources.**
- 2025 edition: Cameroon Tribune calls it the "fourth edition", with the awards night on Saturday 22 Nov 2025 and 21 awardees — [Cameroon Tribune, 2 Dec 2025](http://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/74231/fr.html/cameroon-international-music-festival-entertainment-blends-with-business-in). Aftown reports 50,000+ attendees across Buea and Limbe, 45 artists, 120+ vendors and 300M+ FCFA vendor revenue — [Aftown](https://news.aftown.com/cimfest-2025-bat-des-records/)
- 2023 edition coverage: [Africa24 TV](https://africa24tv.com/cimfest2023-unveiling-cameroons-culture-potential/)
- Not to be confused with CAMIFF (Cameroon International Film Festival) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_International_Film_Festival)

### Inferences
- If the festival has run every year since 2021, 2026 would be its 6th annual festival. The site, however, counts only "four hackathon editions" so far, which suggests the hackathon started around 2022 or 2023 (or skipped a year). CIMFEST26 would then be roughly the 5th hackathon.
- Cliq Empire / Iknite Studio appear to be the digital and production operators. Prince-Michael Enobi is the public face and promoter.

### Gaps
- No legal organizing entity (company or association) was found.
- No authoritative edition count was found; sources disagree.

## What are the themes, tracks, rules, judging criteria and prizes for 2026 (or the most recent edition)?

### Takeaway
CIMFEST26 runs **23–30 November 2026 in Buea and Limbe**. The official hackathon page is still marked "Planned experience · details pending": it publishes no hackathon dates, venue, tracks, team size, criteria, prizes or AI rules, and says "Applications are not open yet" (as fetched 21 Nov 2026, two days before the festival). The best guidance comes from past editions: a 48-hour build on music and creative-industry problems, ending in a pitch, judged on **innovation, feasibility and long-term industry impact** (2025).

### Cited Findings
- CIMFEST26 festival: 23–30 Nov 2026, Buea and Limbe. Music Business Network 23–24 Nov (Buea), Awards 28 Nov, The Wave 29–30 Nov (Mile 6 Beach, Limbe). Programme: Concerts, Awards, Music Business Network, 48-Hour Hackathon, Exhibition, CIMFEST Cares, The Wave — [cimfest.com](https://cimfest.com/)
- Hackathon page (2026): "48-hour tech challenge remains part of the CIMFEST26 experience". Participants go "from a real problem to a working demonstration" on real problems in **Cameroon's creative economy**. Target participants are developers, designers, product builders and founders. Dates and venue "will be published only after official confirmation". "Applications are not open yet." Team size, criteria, prizes, judges and AI rules are not stated — [cimfest.com/experience/hackathon](https://cimfest.com/experience/hackathon)
- Older official copy: "The panel of judges will comprise successful entrepreneurs, tech experts, and renowned figures in the music industry", and mentors guide participants throughout. The aim is to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship in music — [cimfest.com (via search snippet)](https://cimfest.com/hackathon/) (this page now returns 404)
- **2025 hackathon** (Nov 17–19, 2025 per search snippets): 48 hours, 70+ participants (developers, UI/UX designers, engineers, tech and media creators). The mission was to solve real problems in music and entertainment. Prototypes included **AI analysis tools for artists, copyright management and protection systems, digital distribution, fan engagement and ticketing apps, and event logistics systems**. It ended with a pitch session, and the winning team took a **grand prize of 1,000,000 FCFA**, judged on **innovation, feasibility and long-term impact on the industry** — [Aftown](https://news.aftown.com/cimfest-2025-bat-des-records/)
- 2025: 75 young techies from across Cameroon coded solutions to entertainment-industry problems over 48 hours. A tech symposium on innovation and **how AI can help the industry** ran alongside — [Cameroon Tribune, 2 Dec 2025](http://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/74231/fr.html/cameroon-international-music-festival-entertainment-blends-with-business-in)
- Earlier edition (probably 2023, Nov 12–14) theme: **"Hacking Technology-Enabled Solutions to Accelerate Music Promotion, Marketing, and Exportation"**, with 12 teams. Prizes were USD 1,000 / 600 / 400 for 1st/2nd/3rd — [Mountainhub (via search snippets; page now 404)](https://mountainhub.africa/beats-of-innovation-the-48-hours-cimfest-music-hackathon-ignites-a-new-era-for-the-cameroonian-music-industry/)
- A "CIMFEST Hackathon Pitch Template" is on Scribd, which suggests organizers give teams a pitch-deck template. Its content could not be loaded — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/presentation/808168317/CIMFEST-Hackathon-Pitch-Template)

### Inferences
- Expect the same format: about 48 hours over roughly 2–3 days during festival week (past editions ran Nov 10–12, Nov 12–14 and Nov 17–19, early in the festival week), mentors, and a final pitch to a mixed tech, business and music-industry panel.
- The criteria are reported only qualitatively (innovation, feasibility, impact). No weights are known. A working demo plus a credible business and impact story for Cameroonian artists looks essential.
- The 2025 symposium was about AI for the music industry, so AI-powered tools are welcome as product features. That says nothing about whether AI-assisted coding is allowed.
- Applications were still "not open" two days before the festival. The user should confirm directly with organizers (info@cimfest.com, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/cimfest), because the hackathon may be scheduled late in the week, run by registration on the day, or be delayed.

### Gaps
- Not found for 2026: hackathon dates and venue, tracks, team size, eligibility limits (age, nationality, student status), judging weights, prize amounts, IP rules, and **any rule on AI-assisted coding**. There is no Devpost or listing-site page.
- The 2024 hackathon theme and prizes were not found. A 2024 recap listing a Music Hackathon on Nov 10–12 with CAMTEL among sponsors appeared only in a search snippet ([Noirity](https://noirity.com/2024/11/17/test-post/), now 404).

## Who won previous editions, what did they build, and what kind of projects tend to place?

### Takeaway
Only one edition (probably 2023) has documented winners: Team Codec (1st, Bantu Music App), Team Makossa (2nd, "Mutumbu", a distribution and promotion platform for independent artists) and Team Hack'oholics (3rd). In 2024 an Oppnergy team won "Most Innovative Project", sponsored by CAMTEL. The 2025 winner is not named in any source found. Winning projects are consistently **artist-facing music-tech platforms for promotion, distribution and monetization** in Cameroon.

### Cited Findings
- 1st: Team Codec, USD 1,000, Bantu Music App, a music app to help Cameroonian artists promote their work and earn more. 2nd: Team Makossa, USD 600, "Mutumbu", a distribution and promotion platform for independent artists. 3rd: Team Hack'oholics, USD 400 (project not described). The event had 12 teams; the article is dated about late 2023 — [Mountainhub (via search snippets)](https://mountainhub.africa/beats-of-innovation-the-48-hours-cimfest-music-hackathon-ignites-a-new-era-for-the-cameroonian-music-industry/). This is a single source and was not cross-verified.
- 2024: an Oppnergy (Cameroonian hackathon training group) project won "Most Innovative Project" at CIMFEST, sponsored by CAMTEL. The project name and description are not given — [Oppnergy](https://oppnergy.com/hackathon-training-cameroon-oppnergys-approach/)
- 2025 project categories: AI analysis for artists, copyright protection, digital distribution, fan engagement and ticketing, event logistics. The winner received 1M FCFA for innovation, feasibility and long-term impact — [Aftown](https://news.aftown.com/cimfest-2025-bat-des-records/)

### Inferences
- Projects that place solve monetization, visibility and distribution problems for local artists, matching the 2023 theme of promotion, marketing and export. Two of the three documented winners are artist promotion and distribution platforms with local naming and branding (Bantu, Mutumbu, Makossa).
- There is also a separate innovation award (2024), so a technically novel project may win a sponsor or special prize even without a top-3 finish.

### Gaps
- No source explains in detail why winners won, beyond the 2025 criteria.
- The 2024 top-3 and the 2025 winning team are not identified. Mountainhub and Noirity pages now return 404, and LinkedIn posts were not accessible.

## Who are the judges and partners, and what do they care about?

### Takeaway
No named judges were found for any edition. The officially described panel is "successful entrepreneurs, tech experts, and renowned figures in the music industry". CAMTEL (the state telecom operator) is the only documented hackathon sponsor (2024). Universities and ministries (for example MINPOSTEL) have no documented link.

### Cited Findings
- Judge panel described generically — [cimfest.com (via search snippet)](https://cimfest.com/hackathon/)
- CAMTEL sponsored the 2024 "Most Innovative Project" award — [Oppnergy](https://oppnergy.com/hackathon-training-cameroon-oppnergys-approach/). A CAMTEL sponsorship of the 2024 festival also appears in a Noirity recap snippet, now 404 — [Noirity](https://noirity.com/2024/11/17/test-post/)
- CAMTEL runs its own startup competition, the Blue Tech Challenge (winner AGRICFRESH, Oct 2024, Yaoundé), which shows interest in local digital innovation — [Observer237](https://www.observer237.com/2024/10/camtel-blue-tech-challenge-winners.html); [Newswatch Cameroon](https://newswatchcameroon.com/agricfresh-startup-stars-at-maiden-camtel-blue-tech-challenge/)
- "Luxury & Lights by Martel" appears as a sponsored 2025 festival event, not tied to the hackathon — search snippet from [Betatinz](https://betatinz.com/cimfest-2025-a-vibrant-celebration-of-culture-music-innovation-in-cameroon/) (page returned 503)
- 2026 site names no sponsors, only Iknite Studio, Cliq Empire and IkniteOS as design and strategy partners — [cimfest.com](https://cimfest.com/)

### Inferences
- A mixed panel (music industry, entrepreneurs, tech) suggests judges reward clear relevance to artists and fans, business viability in the Cameroonian market (mobile money such as MTN MoMo and Orange Money, low-bandwidth use, local languages), and a live demo. Deep technical novelty alone may matter less.
- With CAMTEL as a past sponsor, telecom and connectivity angles (USSD/SMS, mobile data) may appeal to sponsor judges.

### Gaps
- No judge names or backgrounds were found for any year, and no university or government (MINPOSTEL, MINAC) partnership was found.
- No 2026 hackathon sponsors are listed.
